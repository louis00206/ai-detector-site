Frequently Asked Questions

What is the most accurate AI detector in 2026?

Originality.ai leads with 94% accuracy on GPT-4o content and 92% on Claude 3.5. Winston AI is the closest runner-up at 91% and 89%. Both significantly outperform free tools like ZeroGPT, which scored 84%.

Is GPTZero free?

Yes, GPTZero offers a free tier covering 10,000 characters per month (roughly 1,500-1,800 words). Premium plans start at $10/month on annual billing and go up to $23.99/month for 300,000 words.

Can AI detectors detect Claude and Gemini content?

Yes, but accuracy varies. Originality.ai and Winston AI score 89-92% on Claude 3.5. GPTZero struggles with Claude at only 76% detection. Most top tools now support GPT-4o, Claude, Gemini, and DeepSeek.

Are AI content detectors accurate enough to trust?

For professional use, paid tools like Originality.ai and Winston AI are reliable at 90%+ accuracy. However, false positive rates of 5-15% mean you should treat detection scores as one signal, not proof. Always combine with human review.

What is the best free AI detector?

GPTZero's free tier (10K chars/month) is the most capable free option with 89% accuracy. ZeroGPT offers unlimited free checks but with lower accuracy (84%) and more false positives. Content at Scale provides 25K characters free.

Can AI detectors be fooled?

Yes. Lightly edited AI text drops top detectors' hit rate from 90%+ to 50-70%. Heavily humanized text falls to near chance. This is why detectors should be used as screening tools, not as definitive proof of AI authorship.
